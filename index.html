Core Capabilities

Advanced autonomous systems for Mars exploration

AprilTag Navigation

Uses 25H9 family markers for precise navigation. ID 0 = Stop, ID 1 = Turn Right, ID 2 = Turn Left. Strict model-based detection ensures 99% accuracy.

360° Initial Scan

Complete rotation scan at mission start to locate flags from any starting orientation. Gyroscope integration tracks rotation with high precision.

5-Phase Obstacle Avoidance

Smart avoidance with path memory: Backup → Turn Away → Forward → Turn Back → Return. Maintains original heading after obstacle cleared.

Sample Manipulation

6-phase pickup and 7-phase storage sequences. Dual-finger gripper with 100N force ensures secure sample handling and transport.

Gyro-Integrated Turns

Precise 90° turns using gyroscope integration instead of timed turns. Achieves ±2° accuracy regardless of surface conditions.

Multi-Sensor Fusion

Camera, GPS, Gyro, IMU, Compass, and 5× Distance Sensors provide redundant measurements for reliable autonomous operation.

Team G_Mora Cosmotron

University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka

Our team represents the University of Moratuwa in the Cosmotron Mars Rover Competition 2025. We've designed and developed a fully autonomous rover capable of AprilTag-based navigation, intelligent obstacle avoidance, and precise sample retrieval operations. Our rover showcases the integration of advanced sensor fusion, computer vision, and robotic manipulation systems.
